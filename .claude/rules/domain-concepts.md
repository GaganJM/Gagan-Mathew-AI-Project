# Core Domain Concepts

**Entities**: Customer, Account (new-account onboarding or existing-account
maintenance), Document, DocumentAnalysis, Discrepancy/Deficiency, Reviewer.

**Document categories**:
- KYC/identity: government-issued ID, proof of address, SSN/EIN documentation.
- Business/financial: articles of incorporation, beneficial ownership
  disclosures, financial statements.

**Starting discrepancy taxonomy** (not exhaustive — layer in the bank's actual
onboarding compliance checklist as it's defined):
- Identity mismatches: name/address/date-of-birth inconsistent across
  documents.
- Expired or soon-to-expire identification.
- Illegible, incomplete, or tampered-looking documents.
- Missing documents required for the account type.
- Beneficial ownership inconsistencies versus submitted forms.
- Financial statement inconsistencies: mismatched figures across documents.
